Frequently Asked Questions

Important store details & order guidelines

What is your Return and Exchange Policy?

Please note that we maintain a strict No Return & No Exchange Policy on all items, as all products are heavily discounted branded pieces sold at clearance rates. We encourage customers to verify details on WhatsApp before finalizing orders.

Are the prices negotiable?

No, all prices listed on Kashmim are Strictly Fixed. We offer maximum discounts upfront on original branded clothing to ensure fairness and simplicity for all customers.

Are all products 100% original and authentic brands?

Yes, absolutely! Every article listed in our store is guaranteed 100% original stock sourced directly from recognized brand outlets and authorized clearance inventory.

How do I place an order?

Simply click the "Order via WhatsApp" button under any article. A pre-filled message with the product ID and price will automatically open in your WhatsApp chat so you can send your shipping address to confirm.

What are the delivery charges and estimated timeline?

Delivery typically takes 3 to 5 business days nationwide. Delivery charges are calculated based on parcel weight and destination, which will be confirmed with you on WhatsApp prior to dispatch.
